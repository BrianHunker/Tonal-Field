# Piano samples

These recordings are a subset of the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by **Alexander Holm**
(a Yamaha C5 grand, recorded at 48 kHz / 24-bit with two AKG C414s in AB above the strings),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence (see `LICENSE.txt`).

Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3 — via the SFZ remapping by kinwie,
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

Changes made for this site: 30 notes (every minor third, A0–C8) at 4 of the 16 velocity layers
(v4, v8, v11, v14); mixed to mono; trimmed to 3.5–10 s by register with a fade-out; encoded as 64 kbps MP3.
File names use `s` for `#` (`Ds4v8.mp3` is D#4 at velocity layer 8).
