What happened

How it reads. Every tone counts, however short. In the first phrase the field forms as the max-min reading of its tones and sharpens as they arrive. When two mirror hearings tie, the ring shows the provisional one with the other as a dashed ghost; an arrival that crosses the two tones binding the tie settles it and relabels the phrase — a resolution, not a modulation. After that the field holds nothing but its orientation, set by the last phrase (a phrase ends at a pause). Each new phrase is heard in that orientation; if it places every tone, nothing changes, however it cadences. If it holds a tone that orientation cannot place, the phrase itself decides a new one — as little change as it must — and the field rotates for a one-semitone shift or morphs for anything larger. Non-diatonic colour is not handled yet. Tuning is read from the playing itself.
