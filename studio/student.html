Practice time

Your routine can keep track of how long you practice: the drills and lessons record the time they play, and the stopwatch records its time. With this on, you (and Brian) can see where your practice went, by day, week, month and year, and your daily stars fill by themselves, a tenth at a time, as you play toward each item’s daily minutes. Nothing else is recorded, and you can turn it off at any time.
