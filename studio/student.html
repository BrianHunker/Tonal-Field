Practice time

Your drills can keep track of how long you play along with them — the drill running is your metronome. With this on, each drill you open from this page records the time it plays, and you (and Brian) can see where your practice went, by day, week, month and year. Your daily stars then fill by themselves, a tenth at a time, as you play toward each drill’s daily minutes. Nothing else is recorded, and you can turn it off at any time.
